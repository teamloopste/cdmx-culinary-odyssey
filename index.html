The Journey

Suggested dates: Late December / early January or any weekend you choose. Flexible.

Day 1 — Arrival & Centro Grandeur

Settle in • Historic Center • First flavors
  • Private airport transfer to your Roma Norte boutique (Brick Hotel or Casona Roma Norte).
  • Afternoon: Zócalo, Metropolitan Cathedral, Templo Mayor, and the Diego Rivera murals at Palacio Nacional.
  • Evening: Contramar-style tuna tostadas or a refined first dinner nearby. Early night.

Day 2 — Markets, Street Tacos & Roma Walk

Mercado Roma or San Juan • Orinoco al pastor • Neighborhood discovery
  • Morning market visit (San Juan or Roma) for fresh produce, cheeses, and local ingredients.
  • Legendary al pastor at Orinoco or a top taquería — peanut-aware ordering confirmed in advance.
  • Afternoon: walk Roma Norte’s galleries, design shops, and leafy streets. Optional shopping at the best local stores.
  • Evening: relaxed dinner or mezcal tasting.

Where You’ll Stay

Walkable, safe, beautiful.

Brick Hotel (Top Pick)

Orizaba 95, Roma Norte. Historic mansion (MICHELIN Key), Small Luxury Hotels of the World, Cerrajería restaurant (Chef John Bertrand), speakeasy, spa, rooftop. ~USD 320–450/night double. Perfect central base, walkable to everything.

Casona Roma Norte

Beautiful alternative boutique. Same leafy, safe neighborhood. Strong design, excellent service, and easy Uber access.
